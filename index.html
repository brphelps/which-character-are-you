How the Muppet matching works

The Unified Henson Character Inventory (UHCI) compares your answers with character profiles across six dimensions. Each is a spectrum, not a good side and a bad side.

The six-question quick preview uses one question per dimension. It is provisional: fewer answers mean less detail, and your match may change with the 30-question full profile. Neither is a validated psychological assessment. The Sesame Street quiz is a separate, lighthearted match.
