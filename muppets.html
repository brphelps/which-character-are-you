How this playful match works

Five frequency choices run from Almost never to Almost always. Think about your usual everyday moments, not an ideal version of yourself.

The choices map to 1, 3.25, 5.5, 7.75, and 10 on our existing character scale. This replaces the old uneven 1, 3, 5, 7, 10 spacing; it is a design change, not a calibrated improvement. Reversed questions use 11 minus the answer, so Sometimes stays in the middle.

Each question contributes only to its original primary trait. The preview has one question per trait; full has five. We compare those six averages with the same 15 illustrated Muppet profiles, including Statler and Waldorf as a duo. These fictional baselines and warmer questions are not scientifically validated.
